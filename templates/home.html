FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services and booking process.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book online using the appointment form above, call our clinic directly at +1 (800) CARDIO, or email us at care@cardiocare.com. We confirm all appointments within 2 business hours.

What heart conditions do you treat?

We treat a wide spectrum of cardiovascular conditions including hypertension, coronary artery disease, arrhythmias, heart failure, and structural heart diseases with advanced therapeutic approaches.

Do you offer emergency consultations?

Yes, our emergency care and rapid response cardiac support unit operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week to handle acute heart symptoms and emergencies.

What insurance plans are accepted?

We accept most major health insurance providers. Please contact our front desk or billing department directly to verify whether your specific policy is covered under our network.

How long does a first consultation take?

An initial comprehensive consultation typically lasts between 45 to 60 minutes, which includes a detailed medical history review, physical exam, and discussion of preliminary testing.

Do you offer telemedicine consultations?

Yes, we offer secure virtual telemedicine consultations for follow-up appointments, routine check-ins, and preliminary lifestyle or medication reviews.

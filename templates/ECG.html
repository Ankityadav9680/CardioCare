OUR DIAGNOSTICS

Comprehensive Cardiac Testing

From routine ECGs to advanced imaging, we offer a full spectrum of non-invasive and minimally invasive heart tests.

📈

Resting ECG (EKG)

Quick, painless recording of the heart's electrical activity to detect rhythm abnormalities, blockages, and past heart attacks.

❤️

Stress Test (Treadmill)

Evaluates how your heart responds to exertion, uncovering issues that may not appear at rest.

🩻

Echocardiogram

Ultrasound imaging to visualize heart chambers, valves, and pumping strength in real time.

⏰

Holter Monitoring

24–48 hour continuous ECG recording to catch intermittent arrhythmias that a brief ECG might miss.

💻

Ambulatory BP Monitoring

24-hour blood pressure tracking to diagnose hypertension patterns and guide treatment.

📄

Cardiac MRI / CT

Advanced imaging for detailed anatomy, scar tissue, and coronary artery assessment.

FAQ

Questions About Our Diagnostics

Everything you need to know before your test.

How long does an ECG take? +

A standard resting ECG usually takes about 5 to 10 minutes to perform, while preparation and setup take a few extra minutes.

Are the tests painful? +

No, most of our non-invasive cardiac tests are completely painless and safe, involving little to no discomfort.

When will I get my results? +

Most basic results are ready within 24–48 hours, and urgent findings are prioritized for same-day delivery.

How should I prepare for a stress test? +

Wear comfortable clothing and walking shoes. Avoid heavy meals and caffeine for a few hours before the test as instructed by your doctor.

Do you accept my insurance? +

We work with most major insurance providers. Please contact our front desk with your policy details to verify your coverage.

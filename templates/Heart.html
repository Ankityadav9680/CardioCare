ℹ THE CONSULTATION

What Is a Heart Health Consultation?

A heart health consultation is a comprehensive, one-on-one session with a board-certified cardiologist. It goes beyond a routine checkup — it's an in-depth exploration of your cardiovascular risk profile, lifestyle, family history, and current symptoms. The goal is to detect potential problems early and create a roadmap for prevention or management.

  • ✓ Complete review of medical history & risk factors
  • ✓ Targeted physical examination & baseline vitals
  • ✓ Discussion of diet, exercise, stress, and sleep habits
  • ✓ Personalized preventive strategy & follow-up plan
📋 Your First Visit Includes
  • → Review of symptoms & medical/family history
  • → Blood pressure, heart rate, and oxygen saturation check
  • → Electrocardiogram (ECG) if indicated
  • → Lifestyle assessment (nutrition, activity, stress)
  • → Clear explanation of findings & next steps
